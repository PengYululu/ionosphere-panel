Step 0 · Solar wind IMF & geomagnetic time series

IMF Bz, By (SWMF input, GSM) and OMNI-observed dawn‑dusk E‑field / SYM‑H for the 2024‑10‑10 storm. The dashed red line tracks the snapshot selected on the time‑bar above.

Step 1 · Parcel trajectory map

Trajectories of all traced edge parcels at the 350 km reference altitude, coloured by the selected variable at t0. Click chips below to highlight one or more parcels (bold track, green = start, magenta = end).

Step 2 · Electron density profile along trace

ne vs. altitude for the parcel(s) selected below, one column per traced time step (backward from t0). Reference dotted lines (hmF2 / top / bottom half‑NmF2 heights) are drawn when a single parcel is selected.

Step 2.1 · Electron density time‑height contour

Time–height contour of ne for each parcel selected below (one stacked panel per parcel). Altitudes outside the [bottom_height, top_height] window are masked black; the dotted line traces hmF2(t).

Step 3 · Selected parcel log

Keep a running log of parcels worth revisiting, sorted by geographic region: pick a snapshot with the slider above, type the parcel index (or indices) you care about into whichever region column(s) apply, and add a row. Click any row to jump back to its snapshot, re-highlight those parcels in Steps 1, 2, and 2.1, and load its values back into the boxes below for editing — change them and click "Update row" to save in place instead of creating a duplicate. The table is saved in this browser automatically, and can be exported as a CSV.
